## 🚀 NEW FEATURES YOU CAN USE NOW

### 1. **AI Recommendation Engine** 🤖
**What it does:**
- Gives you personalized eco-action suggestions
- Changes based on festivals (Diwali, Holi, Pongal, Onam)
- Adapts to pollution levels in your city
- Seasonal tips (Summer, Monsoon, Winter)
- Shows how much ₹ and CO₂ you can save

**Example:**
- During Diwali: "Skip firecrackers, save ₹2000 & 50kg CO₂"
- In Delhi winter: "Use metro daily, save ₹800 & 25kg CO₂"
- Summer everywhere: "Set AC to 24°C, save ₹1200/month"

**To see it:**
- Navigate to Recommendations screen
- 10 priority-ranked suggestions
- Beautiful gradient cards
- Tap "Mark Complete" to earn points

---

### 2. **Fitness & Commute Tracking** 🚴‍♂️
**What it does:**
- Tracks how you travel (walk, cycle, bus, metro, EV)
- Automatically calculates:
  - Points earned (15-20 pts/km)
  - CO₂ saved (vs driving a car)
  - Calories burned
- Weekly summaries

**Example:**
- Cycle 5km to work → Earn 100 points + save 1.2kg CO₂
- Take metro 10km → Earn 100 points + save 2.5kg CO₂

**Ready for:**
- Integration with Google Fit / Apple Health
- Step counting with pedometer
- Location-based auto-detection

---

### 3. **Festival Campaigns** 🪔
**What it does:**
- Special eco-challenges during Indian festivals
- 4 major festivals: Diwali, Holi, Pongal, Onam
- Each has 2-4 culturally relevant challenges
- Earn big rewards (50-100 points per challenge)

**Example - Diwali Campaign:**
1. **No Firecrackers Challenge:** 100 points + 50kg CO₂ saved
2. **LED Lights:** 50 points + 15kg CO₂ saved
3. **Clay Diyas:** 75 points + 10kg CO₂ saved
4. **Plant Gifts:** 60 points + 20kg CO₂ saved

**Automatic:**
- Campaigns activate automatically during festival seasons
- Progress tracking
- Bilingual (English + Hindi)

---

### 4. **Team Challenges** 👥
**What it does:**
- Create or join teams (Family, Office, Campus, Neighborhood)
- Compete together towards a goal
- Combine points and CO₂ impact
- Team rankings and achievements

**Example:**
- Family Challenge: "Reduce household waste by 50%"
- Office Challenge: "Carpool days - 100kg CO₂ target"
- Campus Challenge: "Plant 50 trees together"

**Features:**
- Up to 50 members per team
- Real-time progress tracking
- Team vs Team competitions

---

### 5. **Verified Impact** 📸
**What it does:**
- Prove your eco-actions with photos + GPS
- Get verified by community moderators
- Earn trust badges and bonus points
- Anti-fraud protection

**Verification Types:**
- 🌳 Tree planting (photo + GPS)
- 🗑️ Waste cleanup (photo + location)
- 🚗 Carpool events (GPS tracking)
- 🎉 Community events (photo proof)

**Workflow:**
1. Complete eco-action
2. Take photo & allow GPS
3. Submit for verification
4. Get approved → Earn verified badge + extra points

---

### 6. **4-Tier Leaderboards** 🏆
**What it does:**
- See where you rank among others
- Multiple levels of competition

**Leaderboard Types:**
1. **Individual:** Your personal ranking
2. **Team:** Your team vs other teams  
3. **City:** Top contributors in your city
4. **National:** All-India rankings

**Tracks:**
- Total points earned
- Total CO₂ saved
- Number of verified impacts
- Active days/weeks

---

### 7. **Offline-First Mode** 📱
**What it does:**
- Works completely without internet
- Saves everything locally
- Auto-syncs when connection returns
- No data loss ever

**Perfect for:**
- Rural areas with spotty connection
- Traveling users
- Data-saving mode
- Privacy-conscious users

**Technical:**
- Uses SharedPreferences for storage
- Sync queue for pending operations
- Background connectivity monitoring
- Automatic retry on failures
