# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Bhoomi App

Welcome to Bhoomi! This guide will help you get started with the app.

## 📱 Running the App

### First Time Setup
```bash
# Navigate to project directory
cd bhoomi_v1

# Get dependencies
flutter pub get

# Run on your device/emulator
flutter run
```

## 🎯 How to Use the App

### 1. Welcome Screen
When you first open the app, you'll see a beautiful splash screen with the Bhoomi logo. After 3 seconds, you'll be taken to the home screen.

### 2. Home Dashboard
The home screen shows:
- **Your Eco-Score**: A score from 0-100 showing how eco-friendly you are
- **Carbon Footprint Breakdown**: View emissions from Transport, Food, and Energy
- **Quick Stats**: Trees needed to offset your emissions
- **Action Buttons**: Navigate to Calculator, Knowledge Hub, and Community

### 3. Carbon Calculator
Click "Calculate" to enter your data:

#### Transportation Tab
1. Select your vehicle type (two-wheeler, car, auto, etc.)
2. Enter weekly fuel spend (₹) OR weekly distance (km)
3. Set auto/bus usage frequency
4. Click "Save Data" to see your monthly CO₂ emissions

#### Food Tab
1. Slide to set non-veg meals per week
2. Set dairy-free days per week
3. Set local food days per week
4. Click "Save Data" to see impact

#### Energy Tab
1. Select your state (for accurate grid factor)
2. Adjust slider for monthly electricity units (check your bill!)
3. Click "Save Data" to calculate emissions

### 4. Gyan Kendra (Knowledge Hub)
Learn how to reduce your carbon footprint:
- Switch between English and Hindi (EN/हिं button)
- Filter by category: All, Transport, Food, Energy
- Read tips with real savings in ₹ and CO₂ reductions
- Each tip shows practical Indian examples

### 5. Community
Join local sustainability efforts:

#### Local Events Tab
- Browse upcoming events in your city
- See beach cleanups, workshops, plantation drives
- Join events to meet like-minded people
- Track participants and organizers

#### Eco Heroes Tab
- Get inspired by local sustainability champions
- See real CO₂ savings from community members
- Learn from success stories
- Discover sustainable businesses near you

### 6. Profile
View your impact:
- Edit your name and city
- See your day streak 🔥
- Track trees equivalent to your emissions
- View monthly savings in ₹
- Unlock achievements

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Getting Accurate Data
1. **Electricity**: Check your latest electricity bill for exact units (kWh)

## 📊 Understanding Metrics

### CO₂ Emissions
- Measured in **kg (kilograms)**
- Average Indian: ~1,900 kg/year
- App shows monthly emissions for easier tracking

### Trees Equivalent
- 1 tree absorbs ~21 kg CO₂ per year
- Your "trees needed" shows offset requirement
- Plant trees or reduce emissions!

### Savings in ₹
- Calculated from reduced fuel/electricity costs
- Based on average prices in India
- Real money you save monthly!

### Streak Days
- Consecutive days of using the app
- Builds momentum and habit
- Rewards consistency

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Data Not Saving?
- Ensure you clicked "Save Data" button
- Check internet connection (not required, but helps)
- Try restarting the app

### Eco-Score Not Updating?
- Enter data in all three categories (Transport, Food, Energy)
- Click save in each tab
- Return to home screen to see updated score

### Calculator Showing Zero?
- Make sure you entered values in at least one field
- For transport: Enter either fuel spend OR distance
- For energy: Check if you selected correct state
